Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Official Delivery and Shipping Policy

1. Shipping Speeds and Carrier Services:
Hemanth Online Store partners with tier-1 global and domestic carriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL, BlueDart) to ensure prompt, secure package delivery:
- Standard Ground Shipping: 3 to 5 business days across all domestic postal regions. Free for orders exceeding $50.00; otherwise $5.99 flat rate.
- Priority Express Shipping: 2 business days guaranteed. Available at checkout for $12.99.
- Overnight Express Next-Day Delivery: 1 business day for orders finalized before 2:00 PM EST. Available at checkout for $24.99.

2. Order Processing and Handling Time:
Standard warehouse handling and quality verification requires 24 to 48 hours for in-stock merchandise. Orders submitted after 2:00 PM on Friday or over the weekend will commence packaging on the following Monday morning (excluding recognized public holidays).

3. Live Tracking Information:
As soon as your shipment is scanned by the logistics courier at our distribution center, an automated shipping confirmation email and SMS containing your Carrier Tracking Number and live tracking link will be dispatched. Real-time courier tracking updates usually activate within 12 to 24 hours of package handoff.

4. Signature Requirements and Delivery Security:
High-value electronics, luxury merchandise, and orders exceeding $500.00 automatically mandate direct signature upon delivery to prevent porch theft and package loss. If no adult is present to sign for the parcel, the courier will leave a delivery notice and attempt delivery on the next business day.

5. Address Corrections and Lost Packages:
Delivery addresses cannot be updated once a shipment has been dispatched. Please review your street address, apartment unit, and postal code carefully before completing checkout. If a package is marked as "Delivered" by the carrier but cannot be located, please verify around your property and with building neighbors before filing a lost package inquiry within 7 days of the delivery timestamp.
