Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Official Product Return Policy

1. Overview and Return Window:
At Hemanth Online Store, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, eligible items may be returned within 30 days from the confirmed date of delivery. Items returned within this 30-day window are eligible for a 100% full refund to the original payment method or instant store credit.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria:
To qualify for a standard return, items must meet the following criteria:
- Products must be unused, unwashed, and undamaged in original brand-new condition.
- Original manufacturer tags, barcodes, protective seals, and polybags must remain intact and attached.
- All original accessories, instruction manuals, cables, and warranty registration cards must be included in the original retail box.

3. Non-Returnable and Final Sale Categories:
For health, hygiene, and copyright safety, the following items are strictly non-returnable:
- Personalized, custom-engraved, or custom-configured hardware.
- Downloadable digital software licenses and digital gift cards.
- Intimate apparel, innerwear, and hygiene-sensitive wearable accessories.
- Perishable goods or items explicitly marked as "Final Sale - Clearance" at the time of purchase.

4. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items:
If your order arrives damaged during courier transit or contains defective components, please notify our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery. Include high-resolution photographs of the exterior packaging and the damaged item. We will provide an expedited prepaid courier return shipping label and arrange an immediate replacement or full reimbursement.

5. Return Shipping and Processing:
Return shipping is 100% free for all size exchanges or store credit returns. For standard refund requests where the product is not defective, a nominal flat-rate return shipping fee of $4.99 will be deducted from your total refund amount. Once our fulfillment warehouse inspects and scans the returned merchandise, return approval is finalized within 2 business days.
