Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Official Product Warranty Policy

1. Standard Manufacturer Warranty Coverage:
All electronic devices, computer peripherals, smart home appliances, and hardware sold by Hemanth Online Store include a standard 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering hardware defects and manufacturing workmanship from the original date of retail purchase.

2. What Is Covered Under Warranty:
- Mechanical failures and internal component breakdowns during normal everyday usage.
- Defective power adapters, faulty internal display panels, and unresponsive button switches.
- Factory battery defects exhibiting premature failure within 12 months.
- Unrepairable manufacturer defects will be replaced with a brand-new identical or equivalent unit at zero customer cost.

3. Warranty Exclusions and Limitations:
The standard warranty strictly does not cover the following forms of damage:
- Accidental drops, shattered glass screens, or severe physical impacts.
- Water, liquid submersion, or corrosion damage, unless the device possesses an explicit IP68 certification operated within tested boundaries.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs, firmware modifications, jailbreaking, or cosmetic wear and tear (scratches, dents).
- Loss, robbery, or porch theft of the device.

4. Extended Protection Plans (Hemanth Care Plus):
Customers may purchase an optional 2-Year or 3-Year "Hemanth Care Plus" protection plan during checkout. Hemanth Care Plus covers accidental damage from handling (liquid spills, drops), surge damage, and priority 24-hour repair turnarounds with a zero deductible fee.

5. How to File a Warranty Claim:
To initiate a warranty claim, visit our Warranty Service Portal or email warranty@hemanthstore.com with your original Order ID Number, product serial number, and a brief description or short video demonstrating the defect. Our hardware engineering team will provide a prepaid courier return mailer within 24 hours.
