Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Official Refund and Reimbursement Policy

1. Refund Processing Timelines:
Once your returned merchandise is delivered to our central inspection warehouse, our quality control team verifies item condition within 48 to 72 business hours. Upon inspection approval, your refund will be triggered immediately to the original source payment method used during checkout.

2. Settlement Time by Payment Method:
Depending on your financial institution or credit card provider, funds typically reflect in your statement within the following standard windows:
- Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express): 3 to 5 business days after refund issuance.
- PayPal and Digital Wallet Balances: Instant to 24 hours.
- Net Banking and UPI Direct Transfers: 1 to 2 business days.
- Store Credit & Gift Vouchers: Immediately deposited into your Hemanth Customer Account upon approval.

3. Refund Amounts and Deductions:
Refund calculations adhere strictly to the following parameters:
- Full Purchase Price: 100% of the item purchase price, including applicable sales taxes, will be refunded.
- Original Shipping Charges: Standard outbound shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return was necessitated by an order fulfillment error, wrong shipment, or damaged merchandise delivered by our carrier.
- Promotional Discounts: If a bundle discount or coupon code was applied to the order, the refund will reflect the actual discounted amount paid per line item.

4. Partial Refunds and Restocking:
In rare circumstances where returned products exhibit visible signs of personal use, missing packaging accessories, or return postmarking beyond 30 days without prior authorization, a partial refund or a 15% restocking fee may apply at the discretion of the warehouse supervisor.

5. Delayed or Missing Refunds:
If your refund confirmation email has been received but you do not observe the credit after 7 business days, please check with your issuing bank or credit card agency, as processing delays often occur before bank posting. If assistance is still required, contact billing@hemanthstore.com with your Order Reference Number and Transaction ID.
