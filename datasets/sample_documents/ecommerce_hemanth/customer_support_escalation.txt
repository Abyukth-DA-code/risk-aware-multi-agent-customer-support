Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Customer Support and Escalation Policy

1. Multi-Tier Customer Support Structure:
Hemanth Online Store operates an intelligent multi-agent resolution framework designed to provide instantaneous answers to common customer inquiries while seamlessly escalating complex or high-risk cases to designated human specialists:
- Tier 1 (Automated Support): 24/7 AI-driven support chat widget providing immediate grounded guidance on orders, product specifications, returns, and store policies.
- Tier 2 (Human Support Specialists): Dedicated human customer support agents available Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST via live chat and email.
- Tier 3 (Executive Supervisor Escalation): Senior operations and billing supervisors empowered to authorize custom financial overrides, policy exceptions, and executive problem resolution.

2. Criteria for Automated Human Escalation:
Your inquiry will automatically be routed to an authorized human supervisor under any of the following mandatory conditions:
- Fraudulent or disputed financial charges exceeding $100.00.
- Reports of stolen packages, courier delivery fraud, or transit damage claims.
- Formal legal inquiries, regulatory safety notifications, or consumer protection complaints.
- Queries where our knowledge base lacks sufficient authoritative policy evidence to formulate a complete answer.
- Recurring customer dissatisfaction or explicit requests to communicate with a human supervisor.

3. Escalation Tracking and Unique Case Dossiers:
When an escalation event is triggered, the system generates a unique, traceable Escalation Reference ID (e.g., esc_XXXXXXXXXX). An immutable case dossier containing the full customer inquiry transcript, risk tier classification, and safety policy triggers is dispatched to the Human Supervisor Escalation Queue.

4. Supervisor Response Times and Service Level Agreements (SLAs):
- Urgent Financial & Security Inquiries: Reviewed and resolved within 2 to 4 business hours.
- Standard Policy Exceptions and Returns: Reviewed and resolved within 24 business hours.
- Supervisors possess the administrative authority to issue courtesy store credit, authorize manual refund overrides, or expedite replacement shipments.

5. Direct Contact Channels:
- Online Chat Widget: Click the floating chat bubble on any store page.
- Support Email: support@hemanthstore.com
- Telephone Hotline: 1-800-HEMANTH (Available 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM EST)
