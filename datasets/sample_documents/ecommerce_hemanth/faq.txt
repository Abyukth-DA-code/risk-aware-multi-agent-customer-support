Hemanth Online E-Commerce Store - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I track my active order shipment?
A: As soon as your order leaves our fulfillment center, you will receive an email and SMS with a carrier tracking link. You can also view real-time delivery status by visiting your Account Dashboard under "My Orders".

Q2: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Address updates can be accommodated within 30 minutes of checkout by contacting customer support. Once an order enters warehouse packing or transit dispatch, the courier label cannot be altered.

Q3: What should I do if an item arrives damaged or broken?
A: Please photograph the damaged parcel and item within 48 hours of delivery and submit a ticket via our chat widget or email support@hemanthstore.com. We will immediately issue a prepaid replacement.

Q4: How long does it take to receive my refund?
A: Once our inspection center approves your returned merchandise, refunds are released within 24 to 48 hours. Depending on your financial institution, funds appear on your card statement within 3 to 5 business days.

Q5: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we provide international courier shipping to over 65 countries worldwide via DHL Express. International delivery takes 5 to 10 business days. Import customs duties may apply depending on your destination country.

Q6: How do I apply a discount promo code or gift voucher?
A: During checkout, enter your voucher code in the "Have a Coupon?" field on the Payment page and click "Apply". The total cart price will recalculate immediately before payment submission.

Q7: What is your price match guarantee?
A: If an authorized online retailer advertises the exact same in-stock product model for a lower price within 7 days of your purchase, reach out to support@hemanthstore.com with proof to receive a partial refund for the price difference.
