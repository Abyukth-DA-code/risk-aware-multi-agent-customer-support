The following files and directories are **strictly protected** and must remain intact:

5. **Complete Test Suite:**

7. **Environment & Infrastructure Files:**
   - `requirements.txt`, `Dockerfile`, `docker-compose.yml`, `configs/` (`autonomy_rules.yaml`, `domains.yaml`, `model_config.yaml`).
   - `.gitignore`, `.dockerignore`, `README.md`.
